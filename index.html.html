Before you start

What if I miss a day?＋

Every 7-day streak earns you a streak shield. It automatically covers your next missed day, so one busy day doesn't undo weeks of work.

Do I need to already know how to code?＋

No. Tasks start small and build up. The habit matters more than your starting skill level.

Is it free?＋

Yes, free for Indian college students. Just sign in and pick a track.
